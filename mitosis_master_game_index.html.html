Grade 7 Cell Division & Genetics Guide

Cell Cycle, Chromosome Structures & Mitosis vs. Meiosis

The Complete Cell Cycle (Interphase + M-Phase)

  • Interphase (90% of cell life):
    • G1 Phase (First Gap): Cell grows larger and synthesizes organelles.
    • S Phase (Synthesis): DNA replication creates two identical copies of each chromosome.
    • G2 Phase (Second Gap): Final growth and preparation for division.
  • M-Phase (Mitosis & Cytokinesis): Nuclear division ($PMAT$) followed by cytoplasm division.

Chromosome Terminology

Chromatin Loose, uncoiled DNA during Interphase.
Sister Chromatids Two identical strands of a duplicated chromosome.
Centromere The region connecting sister chromatids together.

Mitosis vs. Meiosis Comparison

Feature Mitosis Meiosis
Purpose Growth, Repair, Asexual Reproduction Gamete (Sex Cell) Production
Daughter Cells 2 Genetically Identical Cells 4 Genetically Unique Cells
Chromosome Count Diploid ($2n$) - Full Set Haploid ($1n$) - Half Set

Plant vs. Animal Cytokinesis

Animal Cells: A cleavage furrow pinches the flexible cell membrane inward until it splits into two cells.

Plant Cells: Due to rigid cell walls, vesicles deposit cellulose along the middle to build a rigid cell plate, forming new cell walls.
